Research

Research Areas

Control, planning, and learning for autonomous driving, drifting, and racing at the limits of handling.

Driving

Trajectory planning and control for automated parking, yaw stability, cooperative localization, and distributed vehicle platoons.

  • Guaranteed collision-free planning
  • Two-layer learning MPC for yaw stability
  • Gaussian-process dynamics for platoon control

Drifting

Controllers and planners for aggressive cornering, inertia drift, and safety-aware operation near tire-force limits.

  • Learning-based hierarchical MPC
  • Sparse Gaussian-process control strategies
  • Safety-filtered reinforcement learning

Racing

High-performance racing systems with local trajectory planning, velocity prediction, overtaking, and onboard adaptation.

  • Curvature-integrated MPCC
  • Fast and safe data-driven overtaking
  • F1TENTH competition validation
