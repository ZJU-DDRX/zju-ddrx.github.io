DDRX Vehicle Control Group

DDRX, short for Driving, Drifting, Racing and X, is a vehicle control and autonomous driving team founded in 2023 at Zhejiang University. Guided by Professors Lei Xie and Hongye Su, the team explores advanced control, trajectory planning, autonomous drifting, autonomous racing, and vehicle dynamics at the limit of handling.

About

DDRX 面向自动驾驶、漂移控制、自动竞速与车辆动力学极限操控，围绕车辆轨迹规划、模型预测控制、极限工况车辆操控、自动驾驶系统验证与 F1TENTH 等真实平台开展研究。

Autonomous Driving Automated parking, yaw stability control, cooperative localization, and vehicle platoon control.
Autonomous Drifting Learning-based MPC, Gaussian-process dynamics, iLQR, and aggressive cornering control.
Autonomous Racing F1TENTH racing platforms, MPCC planning, overtaking, and high-speed closed-loop validation.

Research Areas

Safe and Robust Vehicle Control

Model predictive control, safety filtering, stability control, and trajectory tracking for vehicles operating near physical limits.

Learning-Based Planning and Control

Data-driven planning, residual dynamics learning, Gaussian-process models, reinforcement learning, and adaptive MPC for autonomous vehicles.

Autonomous Racing and Drifting

High-performance racing, overtaking, autonomous drifting, aggressive cornering, and real-time planning on scaled and experimental platforms.
