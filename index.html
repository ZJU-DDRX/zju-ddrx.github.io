Mission

From control theory to high-performance autonomy.

DDRX, short for Driving, Drifting, Racing and X, is guided by Professors Lei Xie and Hongye Su at Zhejiang University. The group develops algorithms and systems for autonomous vehicles operating in difficult regimes: aggressive maneuvers, interaction-rich traffic, tire saturation, model mismatch, and real-time compute constraints.

01

Autonomous Driving

Automated parking, yaw stability, cooperative localization, and distributed nonlinear MPC for vehicle platoons.

02

Autonomous Drifting

Learning-based MPC, sparse Gaussian processes, iLQR, and safety filters for vehicles near the limits of handling.

03

Autonomous Racing

MPCC planning, overtaking, onboard reinforcement learning, fast system identification, and F1TENTH validation.

Research Pipeline

Model. Plan. Learn. Validate.

1 Dynamics

Vehicle models, tire behavior, residual learning, and uncertainty-aware prediction.

2 Optimization

MPC, MPCC, iLQR, safety filters, and fast trajectory optimization.

3 Learning

Gaussian processes, reinforcement learning, onboard adaptation, and data-driven planning.

4 Deployment

Simulation, scaled platforms, competition tracks, and real-world closed-loop experiments.
