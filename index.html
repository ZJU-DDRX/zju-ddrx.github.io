DDRX Vehicle Control Group

浙江大学控制科学学院谢磊教授课题组 DDRX 车辆控制小组，面向智能车辆、自动驾驶与机器人系统，研究高性能车辆控制、决策规划、运动预测与真实场景验证。

About

We develop control, planning, and learning methods for autonomous and intelligent vehicles. Our work connects rigorous control theory with real-world driving scenarios, simulation platforms, and experimental validation.

Vehicle Control Trajectory tracking, stability, safety constraints
Autonomous Driving Decision-making, planning, prediction
Embodied Intelligence Closed-loop interaction in dynamic environments

Research Areas

Safe and Robust Vehicle Control

Designing controllers that keep vehicles stable, responsive, and reliable under uncertainty and complex road conditions.

Planning and Decision-Making

Building closed-loop planning algorithms for interaction-rich traffic scenes, lane changes, intersections, and obstacle avoidance.

Simulation and Real-World Validation

Connecting models, datasets, simulators, and vehicle platforms to evaluate algorithms from prototype to deployment.
