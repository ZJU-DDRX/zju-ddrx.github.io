Research Portfolio

Autonomy where control, learning, and vehicle dynamics meet.

DDRX studies intelligent vehicles in regimes where precision matters: tight tracks, low-friction maneuvers, overtaking, drifting, and high-speed closed-loop decision making.

Controllers and planners for aggressive cornering, inertia drift, and safety-aware operation near tire-force limits.
