Research

Projects

DDRX focuses on autonomous driving, autonomous drifting, and autonomous racing, connecting advanced control theory with vehicle platforms and competition scenarios.

Autonomous Driving

Automated parking trajectory planning, yaw stability control, distributed nonlinear MPC for vehicle platoons, and cooperative localization with 5G networks.

Research

Autonomous Drifting

Fast control of drifting states, aggressive cornering, learning-based hierarchical MPC, sparse Gaussian-process dynamics, and ADMM-based iLQR drifting frameworks.

Research

Autonomous Racing

F1TENTH scaled racing, MPCC and curvature-integrated MPCC planning, data-driven overtaking, onboard reinforcement learning, and real-time system identification.
